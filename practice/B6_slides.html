Observe: border-style with 2 values: the first is top and bottom, the second is left and right. So solid double has solid top and bottom edges and double left and right edges. double solid is the reverse. With 3 values: top, then left and right, then bottom. three has solid on top, double on the sides, and groove on the bottom. margin-top: 25%: the gap is 25% of the container's width, not its height. Resize the window and the gap changes. Lists: the ol/ul padding creates the coloured frame. margin-left: 35px on ol li pushes the items right so the numbers show. margin: 5px on ul li puts gaps between the bars.
